The gap

The tools aren’t the problem.

When your AI doesn’t know your brand deeply, it shows. Better prompts won’t fix that, because it’s a context problem, not a prompting problem.

  • Output that doesn’t quite feel like your brand
  • Work that looks different from one channel to the next
  • The same request re-prompted again and again
  • Work that looks like everyone else’s
What it is

A creative director inside the AI you already use.
