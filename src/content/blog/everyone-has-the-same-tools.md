Look across your market right now. Your competitors are using the same handful of models you are. Same tools, same training, same defaults. Everyone got faster at once.

Which means the thing that used to set teams apart, having better tools or using them sooner, has quietly stopped working. You can't win on access to something everyone has.

So why does so much work still look interchangeable? Because most teams are letting the tool decide, and the tool gives everyone the same answer.

## The default is the problem

Ask a model for a landing page, a campaign line, a brand look, and it will give you something competent. It will also give you roughly what it gives the next person who asks. That's not a flaw. It's what an average trained on everything produces. It reaches for the middle, because the middle is where the data is.

Lean on that and your work drifts to the same middle as everyone else's. Not bad. Just indistinguishable. The uncanny feeling of scrolling a feed where every brand sounds like it went to the same school, because in a sense it did.

The teams whose work still looks like theirs aren't using secret tools. They're bringing something to the tool that the tool doesn't have: a point of view strong enough to pull the output away from the default and towards something that's actually theirs.

## Taste is the differentiator now

This is the part that sounds obvious and keeps getting missed. When production is a solved problem for everyone, the scarce thing isn't the ability to make. It's knowing what to make, and being able to tell the distinctive version from the generic one.

That's taste. Judgement. A view on what good looks like for you specifically, held firmly enough to override the easy answer. It was always valuable. It used to be one advantage among several. Now it's close to the only one left that a competitor can't buy off the shelf next week.

The uncomfortable implication is that you can't outsource your way out of sameness by buying more tools. More of the same tools gets you more of the same output. The only move that works is putting stronger judgement into the loop, so the work that comes out carries a fingerprint the model could never have supplied on its own.

Everyone has the same tools. That's precisely why the tools stopped mattering. What you point them at is the whole game now.
